How a garment gets made

Six stages, one thread.

01

Consultation

We talk fabric, occasion, and how the piece needs to live in your closet.

02

Measurement

Upward of thirty points taken by hand, recorded against your posture, not a chart.

03

Pattern & Cut

A block is drafted from scratch, then cut from your chosen cloth on the table.

04

First Fitting

Basted together, tried on, marked up — this is where the real fit gets found.

05

Hand Finishing

Buttonholes, canvas, and hems finished by needle, not machine.

06

Delivery

A final press, a final look, and a garment that's genuinely yours.
